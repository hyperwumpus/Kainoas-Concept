# Kainoa's Hawaiian Grill — Website Concept

An unofficial storefront concept showing how a local restaurant could present its food, personality, and ordering journey through a focused mobile-friendly website.

[View the live concept](https://hyperwumpus.github.io/Kainoas-Concept/#menu)

> This is an independent portfolio concept. It is not the restaurant's official website, and business details should be confirmed before any commercial use.

## The opportunity

A restaurant website should help someone answer three questions quickly: What looks good? Is the business open? How do I order? This concept reorganizes those decisions into a direct path from the hero section to the menu and calls to action.

## Highlights

- Responsive restaurant storefront
- Filterable visual menu
- Clear hours, location, and ordering actions
- Warm island-inspired visual direction
- Single-file prototype that is easy to demonstrate and revise
